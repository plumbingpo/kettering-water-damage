Walking downstairs into standing water is about as stressful as home ownership gets. The source may be a burst pipe, a leaking water heater, seepage through the foundation, a sump pump that quit or drains overwhelmed by a heavy rain, and in each case a fast professional response is what limits mold, structural damage and ruined possessions. Homeowners across Kettering can reach Kettering Restoration 24/7 for emergency water removal, and our crews bring heavy extraction gear and advanced dryers so the basement is safe and dry again without delay.

Limited airflow, below-grade construction and a habit of staying damp make a basement harder to dry than any other part of the house. Between poured concrete underfoot, concrete block on every side and wet soil outside, water has nowhere to go and evaporation on its own is slow. We know those conditions well, so our technicians arrive with truck-mounted extractors for high volumes, submersible pumps for depth, and air movers and dehumidifiers placed strategically to suit a below-grade space.

Getting the water out is step one, and tracking down why the basement flooded is how we help prevent a repeat. Window wells, wall penetrations, exterior grading, foundation drains and sump equipment are all inspected so nothing that played a part gets missed. From there you get our recommendations for lasting fixes, which may include waterproofing, interior drainage or installing a backup sump pump to guard the basement against the next intrusion.

Signs It Is Time for Basement Water Removal

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Musty or moldy odors coming from the basement
  • Water visibly pooled on the floor downstairs, no matter how it got there
  • A sump pump that runs nonstop, does not run at all or makes unusual noises
  • Drywall gone soft and paint flaking off in a finished basement
  • Stained or deteriorating carpet, tile or other basement flooring
  • Leaks at the window frames below grade, or wells holding water outside them
  • Damp or wet walls, especially along the lower part of the foundation

The Benefits of Professional Basement Water Removal

How to Prevent Basement Water Removal Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Four times a year, check that the sump pump works by filling the pit with water
  • Add battery backup to your sump system so it keeps working when the power fails
  • Check that the ground next to the house still slopes away, and regrade where it has settled
  • For seepage that keeps coming back, an interior French drain is worth a look
  • Clean your gutters so they do not overflow right beside the foundation
  • Covers are worth considering for window wells, which also need to stay free of debris
